Getting footage

Capture tips by platform

Consoles

  • PlayStation 5: press the Create button and save recent gameplay, or start a new recording. Move clips to your phone or PC with the companion app or a USB drive.
  • Xbox Series X|S: press the Xbox button, open Capture & share, and choose "Record what happened." Your clips sync to the Xbox app.
  • For the sharpest results, set console capture to the highest resolution it offers. ClipHeist detects the frame rate automatically and keeps it.

PC & capture cards

  • Instant replay: NVIDIA and AMD driver overlays can save the last few minutes with a hotkey.
  • OBS replay buffer: record at a high bitrate (40+ Mbps for 1080p60) so your clips still look good after the platforms re-compress them.
  • ClipHeist opens anything your browser can play: MP4 (H.264 or HEVC), WebM, and most MOV files.
Stay claim-free

Share gameplay the right way

Most gameplay clips are fine to post. Licensed music is what usually gets them claimed. A few habits keep your channel safe.

Watch out for

  • In-game radio and licensed songs. These often trigger automatic Content ID claims or muted audio.
  • Leaked or pre-release material. Only post footage from the retail game you actually played.
  • Official logos and key art in thumbnails, if they make your clip look like an official release.

Do this instead

  • Use Mute game audio or lower the music, and add your own royalty-free track.
  • Add commentary, captions or a reaction. Original, transformative content also performs better.
  • Read the publisher's current video and monetization policy before you monetize.
  • Make thumbnails from your own gameplay frames. The Studio has a one-click snapshot tool.
FAQ

Questions, answered

Is ClipHeist really free? Is there a watermark?

Yes, it's free with no watermark. The only text on your video is the creator handle you choose to add.

Do my videos get uploaded to your servers?

No. Decoding, editing and rendering all happen on your device. A file only leaves your device when you choose to post it, for example to YouTube from your own account.

Which browsers work best?

Use a current desktop version of Chrome or Edge. They export MP4 (H.264), which every platform accepts. Firefox exports WebM, which YouTube and X accept. Safari support varies by version. Rendering runs in real time, so keep the tab visible while it works.

Will my clip keep its frame rate?

Yes. In Chrome and Edge, ClipHeist decodes every frame of your recording with WebCodecs and writes an MP4 at the exact frame rate of your footage: 30, 59.94, 60, and so on. It's not a real-time screen capture, so a busy computer can't drop frames. Variable-frame-rate recordings from phones and consoles are converted to a smooth constant rate. Browsers without WebCodecs fall back to real-time capture.

Can I post directly to TikTok or Instagram?

Those apps don't let websites post for you without approval, so ClipHeist takes the next-fastest route. It saves the file, copies your caption, and opens the upload page. On mobile, the Share button sends the clip straight to the app.

How does AI Edit work? Does it upload my video?

The analysis runs on your device. It measures loudness, sudden impacts, motion, flashes and scene cuts across your whole recording, scores every moment for the style you pick, and builds the edit. Your video is never uploaded. If the site owner turns on the optional cloud writer, about six small still frames are sent to write your title and captions. Otherwise, captions are written on your device.

Can I add my own commentary?

Yes. Open the Audio tab and press Record voice-over (or press V). The edit plays while you talk, and your take is placed exactly where you recorded it. Game audio and music dip automatically while you speak. For the cleanest sound, use headphones.

Is this an official GTA product?

No. ClipHeist is an independent tool that works with footage from any game. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by any game publisher, and it contains no game assets.
