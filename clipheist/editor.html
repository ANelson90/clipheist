      The frame-exact engine mixes audio offline at 48 kHz, so it stays in sync with every video frame.

      Avoid copyright claims: in-game radio and licensed songs are the most common cause of Content ID claims and muted audio. For safer uploads, mute or lower the game audio and add royalty-free music you have the rights to use.
